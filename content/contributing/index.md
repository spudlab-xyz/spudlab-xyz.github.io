title: Contributing

Thank you for considering a contribution. Spudlab welcomes improvements to software, hardware, firmware, documentation, accessibility, testing, and scientific practice.

The [canonical contribution guidance](https://github.com/spudlab-xyz/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) is maintained in the Spudlab organisation repository. Guidance in a specific project takes precedence over these defaults.

## Before starting

- Read the target repository's README, licence, and project-specific setup, coding, hardware, and testing instructions.
- Discuss substantial changes in an issue before implementation so the problem, scope, and compatibility effects are understood before significant work begins.
- For security concerns, follow the target repository's `SECURITY.md`; do not open a public issue.

## Making a change

Keep pull requests small and focused. Explain the problem addressed, the approach taken, and any relevant trade-offs.

Validate the change meaningfully and in proportion to its risk. Follow project-specific checks and describe what was and was not tested. Consider documentation, accessibility, compatibility, data migration, and hardware implications.

Changes affecting measurements, scientific interpretation, data transformations, or other scientific behaviour should include evidence for the approach and validation. Record assumptions, units, provenance, uncertainty, and known limitations where relevant. Follow the [scientific principles](/scientific-principles/).

Reviews may question an approach rather than the contributor. Be respectful, explain reasoning, respond constructively, and allow disagreement to improve the result.

## Safe and lawful contributions

Only submit material that you have permission to contribute under the target repository's applicable licence. Preserve required attribution and third-party notices.

Never include credentials, access tokens, personal data, private station details, or data and fixtures restricted by a provider's terms in issues, commits, or pull requests. Use minimal synthetic examples where possible and redact diagnostics carefully.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is provided under the licence and contribution terms stated by the target repository. No additional copyright assignment or sign-off is required unless that repository explicitly says otherwise.
