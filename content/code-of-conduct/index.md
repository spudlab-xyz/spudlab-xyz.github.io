title: Code of Conduct (draft)
description: Current status of the draft Spudlab Code of Conduct.
---

Spudlab has prepared a draft Code of Conduct based on the Contributor Covenant, but **it is not active and must not yet be relied upon as published policy**.

## Publication blocker

The draft cannot be activated until Spudlab approves and tests a monitored private contact for conduct reports. The enforcement section still contains a placeholder for that contact.

Do not submit conduct concerns through a public issue. Until an appropriate private reporting route is established, GitHub's own [reporting and blocking tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) may be available where applicable.

## Draft development

The draft sets an intention to foster an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community. It outlines expected behaviour, unacceptable behaviour, scope, enforcement responsibilities, and potential consequences, but none of those provisions should be represented as an active Spudlab process while the publication blocker remains.

Read the [canonical draft](https://github.com/spudlab-xyz/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) or [propose an update](https://github.com/spudlab-xyz/.github/edit/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) on GitHub.
