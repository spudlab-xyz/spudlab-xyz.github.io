Spudlab builds open-source software and hardware that make scientific data easier to collect, preserve, understand, and share.

## How we work

We work in the open and value scientific integrity, accessible design, and reproducible methods. Our [scientific principles](/scientific-principles/) explain how we make data traceable, preserve measurement context, document methods, and correct mistakes visibly.

## Get involved

Contributions are welcome across code, documentation, testing, accessibility, scientific review, calibration, hardware experiments, and constructive feedback. Read the [contribution guidance](/contributing/) before getting started, then visit [Spudlab on GitHub](https://github.com/spudlab-xyz) to explore the work and take part.

Spudlab also has a [draft Code of Conduct](/code-of-conduct/). It is not active because a monitored private reporting contact has not yet been approved; the status page explains this publication blocker and must be read before relying on the draft.
